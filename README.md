# 🚀 Evolving Workforce Architect: Predictive Modeling & Intelligence for the AI-Driven Job Market


## 📌 Business Overview
Modern corporate leaders and HR strategists struggle to balance aggressive talent investments against rapid automation risks. This end-to-end data analytics project engineers a raw, messy dataset of **1,545 job market records** into an executive business intelligence suite. 

The pipeline normalizes structural workforce categories, repairs missing salary benchmarks, maps skill densities, and deploys a **Machine Learning Engine** to predict role vulnerability based on organizational traits.

---

## 🛠️ Tech Stack & Architecture
* **Environment:** Google Colab (Python 3.x runtime)
* **Data Engineering & ML:** `pandas`, `numpy`, `scikit-learn`
* **Data Extraction:** `sqlite3` (SQL relational environment mapped in-memory)
* **Business Intelligence:** Tableau Desktop / Public
